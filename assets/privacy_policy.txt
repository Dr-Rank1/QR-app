# QR Studio Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

## Data Collection

QR Studio respects your privacy. Here's what we collect:

### Local Data (Stored Only on Your Device)
- **Scan History**: QR codes you scan are stored locally using Hive database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.
- **Generated QRs**: Codes you create are saved locally in My QRs.
- **Settings**: Your preferences (theme, sound, vibration) are stored locally.

### Camera Access
- QR Studio requires camera access to scan QR codes.
- We do not take photos or record video.
- Camera frames are processed locally and discarded immediately.

### Advertising
- This app displays ads through **Start.io** (StartApp).
- Start.io may collect device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, and similar technical data to serve and measure ads.
- You can learn more at [Start.io Privacy Policy](https://www.start.io/policy/).

## Data Usage

We use collected data to:
- Provide QR scanning and generation features
- Save your scan history for reference
- Display and measure in-app advertising

## Data Sharing

- Scan history and generated QR content stay on your device unless you export or share them.
- Advertising data may be shared with Start.io and its ad partners to deliver ads.

## Your Rights

- **Delete Data**: Clear scan history or My QRs from Settings
- **Withdraw Consent**: Uninstalling the app removes all local data

## Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@example.com

## Children's Privacy

QR Studio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
